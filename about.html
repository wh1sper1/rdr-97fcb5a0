About the Daily Anti-Fascist Brief

Independent · sourced · updated twice daily

The Daily Anti-Fascist Brief is an independent news page built for one purpose: to defeat fascism and the corruption of politicians. Anti-fascism is the simple idea that authoritarian movements should be opposed before they take power — and this page does that with a shovel-load of evidence instead of slogans: every claim we publish is dated and linked to the original article, so you can check the record yourself.

What this page covers

  • Antifa and the anti-fascist movement — protests, crackdowns, government designations and court cases, reported from both sympathetic and critical outlets with every claim attributed.
  • Political corruption and anti-corruption reporting — graft, conflicts of interest, pay-to-play, and the corporate interests and money riding on political office: the anti-corporate abuse that political power enables.
  • Geopolitics, the Ukraine-Russia war, Israel's wars and cyber operations, and a world report catch-all for everything else moving the planet.
  • Cyber security, surveillance overreach, AI, and laws touching 3D printing and CNC manufacturing.
  • Culture sections too: gaming and synth music — what scenes are actually buzzing about right now.

How it works

Research runs twice a day — 3 AM and 6 PM Pacific. Each brief gathers the last 48 hours of reporting, attributes every fact to its publication, and links straight to the source. No paywall, no ads, no trackers, no data collection. If the work is worth something, use the donation code below — it keeps the page independent.
