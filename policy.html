// Where the radar is blind
  • Refused, not missing. Germany, Ireland, Israel, New Zealand and Australia all publish their bills — their sites refuse automated readers, so this page cannot reach them. That is the biggest hole here, and it is a wall, not a void.
  • Depth varies by parliament. Congress and the EU are swept whole — every bill in the current Congress, every live EU procedure. State legislatures are scanned on a rolling weekly cycle, each one once every seven days, so a state line here can be up to a week old. Elsewhere we can only reach what each parliament chooses to publish: France and Canada give us their latest deposits, India and South Africa their full current tracks, Japan its sitting docket.
  • Silent parliaments. Others publish no machine-readable docket at all — no feed, no export, nothing a reader or this page can check. We would rather show the gap than paper over it with a guess.
