Key findings

The M-VAVE FM-1 is the synth of the moment

  • gearnews (Oct 7): "The M-VAVE FM-1 frenzy has sound designers jumping onboard" — the ~$60-70 DX-7-style pocket FM synth is "truly the synth of the moment right now," with commercial sound designers rushing out preset packs while demand surges. (Source)
  • Synth Anatomy (Oct): M-VAVE released free firmware V15 for the FM-1 — init voice creation, voice rename/copy, operator mute rules, finer envelope parameter ranges (1-100), two-mode glide, longer sequencer notes, per-pattern voice binding, and note-on velocity over MIDI. The 12-voice, six-operator, 32-algorithm DX-7-compatible synth sells around $70 on AliExpress/Temu (~€100 Amazon DE). (Source)
  • Synth Anatomy (Jul 15 review): Hardware verdict — full DX-7-style 12-voice engine, six effects, arpeggiator/sequencer, 12-hour battery, USB-C audio interface, Bluetooth MIDI, built-in speaker; cons: mono engine despite stereo jack, 16-step sequencer, TRS-MIDI adapter not included. (Source)

Firmware war: four custom OSes in 48 hours

  • TimeToHouse (Oct): Four independent developers shipped competing custom firmwares for the FM-1 within 48 hours: Groove OS ($29, Peter Gombos — 8-track groovebox, four synthesis engines, 64-step sequencers, parameter locks), SLOOP (free/open-source, 3dSam, Oct 5 — 4-track groovebox), Felucca (Hugelton Instruments, free/open-source — 13 engines incl. ports of Mutable Instruments' open Eurorack designs), and X0X (free beta by Charles Vestal, creator of Propellerhead's ReBirth — TR-909/TR-808 drums plus TB-303 acid). Synth Anatomy first reported the collision Oct 4. None sanctioned by M-VAVE — all reverse-engineered. (Source)
  • MatrixSynth (Oct 18): SLOOP 2.6 released today — punch-in effects, track insert effects, texture features, iPad recording workflow. (Source)
  • MatrixSynth (Oct 16-17): MYCELIUM 1.0 (Oct 16) — free open-source firmware for Forest/Psytrance/Goa/Hitech grooves by Lars Knacken, built on Felucca + SLOOP, public GitHub release. Skiff 1.3 (Oct 17) — six-track groovebox, second core in beta (12 voices at once), MIDI learn, per-track insert effects. Also HYP-1 (hypnotic techno/acid OS) and FM-1 NOIZ DEMONS OS by SPIKE the Percussionist. (Source)
  • GrooveNews (Oct 18): fm1-emu — a browser-based FM-1 emulator (Joakim Eriksson) lets anyone play the synth and its custom firmwares without hardware. Same day: M-VAVE launched "FM-1 Open," an official channel for alternative-firmware developers — the company is embracing the modding scene it didn't sanction. FoMni firmware turns the FM-1 into a pocket Suzuki Omnichord. (Source)
  • Hacker News (Oct 8): Reverse-engineering thread hits the front page — "A TON of custom firmwares have shown up… $60! It is VERSATILE: new firmwares are released almost every week now. It is HACKABLE: yes kids, music technology should always be hackable." (Source)

Also trending in synth gear

  • GrooveNews (Oct 18): Behringer JN-80 (Juno-60 clone) open for EU pre-order; Moog Matriarch reportedly discontinued per dealers; Teenage Engineering OP-XY firmware 1.2 adds drum synth + multichannel USB audio; Twisted Electrons FLEXFM (8-voice hybrid groovebox) pre-order; Beads granular module fully open-sourced. (Source)
