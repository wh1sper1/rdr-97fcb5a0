The Trump Stack

Who pays, who gets paid: the money, the titles and the family business around one president · data as of October 2026

This is not a conspiracy chart. It is a cap table. Everyone on this map is doing one of three things: paying Donald Trump, being paid by Donald Trump, or laundering one into the other. The money loop is simple: venture billionaires fund his campaign, his government hands their portfolio companies the contracts, the contract revenue funds the next campaign. The only genuine free radical here is John McAfee, and the system's answer to him was a prison cell in Barcelona.

    1 · The defense loop

    Thiel, Andreessen and Luckey co-own the same weapons company. Anduril collects $2.9B to build submarine parts and $1.9B for command software under Trump, while Trump personally goes to Baltimore to read the press release. The revenue then funds the next round of donations. It is not a market. It is a faucet, and they installed the valve.

    2 · Murdoch sells the customer, Trump is the product

    Fox paid $787.5M to settle Dominion's defamation case, and Murdoch testified under oath that he knew the 2020 election was fair while his hosts said otherwise. The memo in evidence: we don't want to antagonize Trump further. The network is not his propaganda arm. It is his HR department: Hegseth, Bongino, Gorka, Huckabee all left the TV set for the government paycheck.

    3 · McAfee is the control group

    Same anti-state mythology, same crypto, same Twitter pumps, same promised deep-state enemy. Difference: no Founders Fund, no PAC, no pardon pipeline. The SEC caught his $23M coin-pump racket and hours after a Spanish court approved extradition he was dead in his cell. The mythology is sold to the audience and cashed out by the men on this map. McAfee believed it.

    4 · JFK is the currency, Trump is the bank

    The Kennedy assassination myth is the longest-running bestseller in American conspiracy culture. Trump monetized it with one signature: EO 14176, ordering full release of the JFK, RFK and MLK files. RFK Jr. thanked him publicly and took HHS. After the Butler rally fist photo, the imagery wrote itself, and the campaign logged its best small-dollar day. Dead president as loyalty program.
