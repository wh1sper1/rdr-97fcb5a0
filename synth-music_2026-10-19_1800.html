Key findings

🔥 TRENDING: M-VAVE FM-1 & the Custom Firmware Gold Rush

  • Gearnews (Oct 7, 2026): The M-VAVE FM-1 is truly the synth of the moment right now, with sound designers making hay while the sun shines. A new browser-based emulator called fm1-emu by Joakim Eriksson lets producers play the M-VAVE FM-1 synthesizer and explore custom firmware without owning the hardware — fueling the exploding third-party mod scene. (Source)
  • Push and Groove (Oct 19, 2026): Elektron has officially responded to the growing wave of alternative firmware for its instruments — a hot topic in the synth community. Unofficial OS builds can change sequencing, sound engines, and overall behavior of hardware that many producers rely on in studio and on stage. The discussion matters because it touches on the line between user freedom and manufacturer control. (Source)
  • Gearnews (Oct 15, 2026): Three new third-party hacks dropped for hardware: custom firmware for Sequential Prophet-5, Arturia MicroFreak, and Novation Circuit Tracks. Custom firmware is still booming across the synth world, with hackers pushing the boundaries of what commercial instruments can do. (Source)

🔥 TRENDING: Teenage Engineering OP-XY Firmware 1.2 & Major Hardware News

  • Push and Groove (Oct 19, 2026): Teenage Engineering released firmware 1.2 for the OP-XY flagship groovebox, adding a new drum synth engine, multi-channel USB audio, a dynamic filter, expanded envelope controls, and detune. The update expands the instrument rather than replacing it — a significant addition for the sequencer-first workflow device that launched nearly two and a half years ago. (Source)
  • Synthtopia (Oct 19, 2026): Twisted Electrons announced its FLEXFM synth and sampler is now on preorder — featuring eight independent tracks, four operators per voice, over 30 operator engines, and three ways to morph sounds, with each voice having its own multimode analogue filter. First LOVE FM is ready to order in two delicious colors, nearly two and a half years after debuting at Superbooth 24. (Source)
  • Push and Groove (Oct 19, 2026): Dealers worldwide are indicating the Moog Matriarch semi-modular analog synthesizer has been discontinued — a significant loss for the semi-modular analog community. Behringer's JN-80, an eight-voice analog clone of the Roland Juno-60, is now available for pre-order in the EU, featuring the familiar Juno-style architecture and BBD chorus. Waldorf released free Chutney librarian software for its compact Protein wavetable synth. (Source)

New Synth Gear & Software Releases

  • Synthtopia (Oct 19, 2026): Synthesizers.com introduced the Q215 Digital Reverb — a multi-mode stereo reverb for MU modular systems. Midicake UNA is a hardware MIDI performance sequencer that uses the chords you play to generate coordinated chord, melody, and bass parts for external synthesizers. Oddment's ODD-1 hardware instrument combines granular synthesis, real-time audio processing, and synchronized looping around a shared microsonic engine. (Source)
  • Piano & Synth Magazine (Oct 2026): Rob Papen released QUAD-2, the second version of his phase distortion and wave shaping virtual synth, with a new interface, Ribbon Controller, multiple filter types, FX, and more. UJAM launched Beatmaker BOUNCE and Usynth BOUNCE — both inspired by late-'90s and early-2000s R&B, hip-hop, and club music. Toybox Audio updated Buzz Zone to version 3.0, a free major update for the hybrid wavetable synth on macOS, Windows, and iPadOS. Native Instruments released Absynth 6.2, a free update focusing on workflow improvements. (Source)
  • Synthtopia (Oct 2026): Shear Electronics' The Relic pairs eight independent discrete analog voice cores with four-part multitimbrality, extensive modulation, and a continuously variable filter architecture. Våld Labs launched a free public beta for its Ravik generative drum synth app/plugin — eight voices, 47 synth engines, nine generative algorithms. Dubreq miniaturized the iconic Stylophone even further with the launch of the Mini. (Source)

Album & Single Releases — Synthpop, Synthwave, Darkwave

  • SYNTHPOPWORLD (Oct 19, 2026): Copenhagen dark synthwave artist St. Digue released the third album Secret Language, led by the singles Relapse. Athens synthpop duo Ekospell returned with the analog single Creatures Of The Night and its official video. Swedish synthpop duo Flare Dimension released their debut album Sad Girl Sad Boy on Radiation Street Records. German futurepop project digital ENERGY released Echoes Of Tomorrow with remixes by Funker Vogt and Nuquatron. (Source)
  • SYNTHPOPWORLD (Oct 17–18, 2026): Honduran synthwave producer Paradise86 returned with Girl with Glasses, a two-track single. Munich darkwave duo Rue Oberkampf returned with Wehmut, six songs of forest, sea, and longing on Young Army Records. Polish synthwave duo Cassetter & Cyberwalker expanded After The Rush with eight new remixes. Kid Neon's eight-track Digital Bouquet arrived on My Pet Flamingo with City Lights. Belgian synthpop acts Infrablack and Nevel released No Angel, reworking a 2001 Propulsion track. (Source)
  • SYNTHPOPWORLD (Oct 2026): The Human League's Dare — the Martin Rushent-produced synthpop landmark — turns 45 on October 16. Swedish dark synth act Priest set their fifth album Altered State Police for October 23 on Blue Heart Records. Amsterdam darkwave artist Subject Sue pressed debut EP Details In The Dark to limited Avant! vinyl. DROIDGLOW returned to NewRetroWave with Brutal Hours, an eleven-track darksynth album featuring guests including House of Ash. Apoptygma Berzerk's 2016 instrumental album Exit Popularity Contest finally arrives on download and streaming services on October 27. (Source)
  • Side-Line (Oct 2026): Hermidgets, the anonymous Italian dark synthpop project, released 'Like Moths' alongside an official clay-mask video. Twin Ritual released 'Just Like A Vampire.' Split Vision released 'Cold War,' the second single from BetaCity. De/Vision, the German synth-pop duo of Steffen Keth and Thomas Adam, released their sixth studio album Two on October 1. Assemblage 23 released 'Believe (INHALT Remix)' on Metropolis Records. (Source)
