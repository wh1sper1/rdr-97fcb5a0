Key findings

War operations — Gaza

  • Times of Israel (Oct 16-17): IDF strikes killed two Hamas terrorists who invaded Israel on Oct. 7, 2023 — Nukhba Force cell commander Maher Walid Omar al-Husseini (attacked Re'im base) and weapons-production commander Mohammed Mohammed Abd al-Khalek Hala — plus Palestinian Islamic Jihad operative Ahmad Jabrin Tawfiq Dalha in Khan Younis. IDF says all were "advancing attacks" and restoring capabilities "in systematic violation of the ceasefire."
  • AP (Oct 8): Israeli strikes following warnings in Gaza City and southern Gaza killed two, including a child; a strike on a house in the Sabra neighborhood killed a woman and child and wounded 10.
  • Le Monde: Gaza rescuers say Israeli fire killed seven; the army said three were "military operatives in terrorist organizations." Israel says it killed a Turkish NGO director in a Gaza strike, accusing him of Hamas ties "without providing evidence."
  • Le Monde (with Breaking The Silence): Israeli officers described Gaza rules of engagement, the acknowledged number of civilian casualties, and the extensive use of artificial intelligence — one officer calling it a "'factory of targets' and 'collateral damage.'"
  • World Israel News: Israel reportedly blocking construction of a military base in Gaza for "Board of Peace" forces ahead of the Knesset election; Israel has reportedly withdrawn from Gaza's Orange Line to the original Yellow Line under US pressure, reducing control from 60%+ to 53%.

War operations — Lebanon

  • AP (Oct 8): An Israeli officer was killed and three soldiers injured when their vehicle overturned during an operation in the Litani area. Troops remain in parts of southern Lebanon despite US-mediated withdrawal talks; Hezbollah has refused to disarm.
  • Le Monde: Four killed by Israeli airstrikes in Lebanon despite the ceasefire; Lebanon's president called on the US to stop the strikes. Israel said it destroyed key Hezbollah tunnels in south Lebanon containing "dozens of Iranian-made rockets, missiles, and UAVs" plus anti-tank missiles and hundreds of mines.
  • Ground News / IDF (Oct): Israeli Air Force struck 500+ targets in the past month — 370+ Hezbollah targets in southern Lebanon, 140+ in Gaza, including three Hamas brigade commanders.
  • World Israel News: Israel threatened to seize more of Lebanon and displace over one million Shiites if Hezbollah joins future Iranian attacks on Israel. Hezbollah launched explosive drones at IDF troops in the security zone; one downed.
  • JFeed (Oct 16): Northern Israeli councils warned of planned air force strikes in the Lebanon sector.

Cyber & covert operations

  • WSJ (Oct 10, via Ground News): Nations including Russia, Ukraine, Israel and Iran are increasingly hacking internet-connected surveillance cameras for wartime intelligence — ~1.2 billion cameras worldwide, weak passwords and outdated firmware. Israel National Cyber Directorate chief Yossi Karadi said Iranian operatives infiltrated road cameras during the June 2025 conflict to track Israeli figures and access the Weizmann Institute. A henhouse camera in northern Israel exposed Israeli military movements near the Lebanon border (its app was found in Arabic). Ukraine's SBU has secured 40,000 compromised cameras since 2022.
  • Daily Beirut / Channel 12 (Oct 2026): Hezbollah has attempted 600+ cyberattacks against Israel since Oct 7, aided by AI, including during ceasefires — Israeli military says no significant successes so far. More than 2,500 cyberattacks against Israel since Oct 7, countered by the Electronic Defense Brigade.
  • JNS (archive): Iranian hackers claimed to have breached the Dimona nuclear facility, claiming thousands of documents. Iran blamed Israel for the Fars News Agency hack — group "Black Reward" claimed ~250 TB deleted.
  • Israel National News (archive): Lebanon's parliamentary committee claimed Israeli spying devices infiltrated UNIFIL and Lebanese army networks. A Knesset website was targeted by Russian hackers with alleged Kremlin ties ("XakNet Team"), claiming revenge for Israeli intel sent to Kyiv on Iranian-made drones.

Politics

  • CNN (Oct 18): Nine days before Israeli elections — the first general election since the Oct 7 attacks — Trump finally endorsed Netanyahu. Israel marked the third anniversary of Oct 7 with tributes, Gaza strikes, and political debate. Defense Minister Israel Katz ordered increased military vigilance.
