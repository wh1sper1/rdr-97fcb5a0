Netanyahu's UNGA speech and the October 27 election

  • PBS NewsHour (September 2026): Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu addressed the UN General Assembly, calling allegations of Israeli genocide in Gaza "the biggest lie of the century." He asserted Israel's right to wage war against Iran, saying attacking Iran "was one of the easiest decisions I've ever had to make," and denounced as "moral cowards" the delegates who walked out before he began speaking. Netanyahu held up a pager as a reference to the September 17, 2024, remote detonation of thousands of pagers distributed to Hezbollah, which wounded more than 3,000 people and killed 12, including two children. He dismissed West Bank settler violence as "a handful of young juvenile delinquents" and reiterated that no Palestinian state will be established while he is Israel's leader. (Source)
  • Middle East Eye (October 2026): The Israeli election on October 27 is described as one of the most important in the country's history — the first since 2022 and the Hamas-led attacks of 2023, the genocide case in Gaza, and the war on Iran. Netanyahu, Israel's longest-serving premier and now seeking a seventh term, faces wide criticism for his handling of the wars, which have isolated Israel internationally. Tens of thousands of Israelis remain displaced amid attacks from Hezbollah in Lebanon. The government is seeking to push ahead with the E1 settlement project east of Jerusalem, which will divide the West Bank. Netanyahu's current government, in power since 2022, is the most right-wing in Israeli history and has overseen what the ICC has characterized as genocide and war crimes in Gaza, for which Netanyahu faces an arrest warrant. (Source)

The Iran wars and regional military operations

  • Wikipedia — Benjamin Netanyahu (October 2026): In February 2026, Israel and the US launched a major military offensive against Iran with the stated goal of inducing regime change. According to multiple diplomatic sources, Netanyahu lobbied President Trump to initiate the joint strikes. In April 2026, Netanyahu said "There is no ceasefire in Lebanon. We are continuing to strike Hezbollah with full force." In July 2026, he said the Gaza Strip was "no longer" a military threat to Israel. The 2026 Iran war, launched alongside the US with the stated goal of regime change, followed Israel's 2025 airstrikes on Iran's nuclear facilities. The joint US-Israeli strikes assassinated Iranian Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei and other officials. Netanyahu cited his ongoing corruption trial as evidence for the need for further judicial reform. (Source)
  • Times of Israel (October 2026): In a pre-election briefing, Netanyahu vowed to bring down the Iranian regime, downplayed the level of West Bank settler violence despite mounting international backlash, and warned that the election could determine whether Israel completes its military objectives or returns to "failed security policies." His updated national security doctrine prioritized preemptive offensive action over containment, arguing "We changed the Middle East because we changed ourselves." On Gaza, Netanyahu reiterated Israel would not permit reconstruction before Hamas was disarmed and the territory demilitarized, despite the Trump administration pushing to advance its 20-point plan. On Lebanon, he said Israel had allowed the Lebanese army to demonstrate its ability to clear Hezbollah infrastructure in two pilot areas but it had "not demonstrated sufficient capabilities." The presentation identified both the Iran-led Shiite axis and the Turkey-led Muslim Brotherhood axis as major regional challenges, alongside emerging threats including precision-guided missiles, drones, unmanned maritime systems, cyberwarfare, AI, and quantum technologies. (Source)

Intelligence failures and Unit 8200

  • Times of Israel Blogs: Three years after October 7, serious questions remain about the decisions that preceded the massacre, the prolonged conflict in Gaza, and policies creating new threats. Israeli intelligence obtained Hamas's detailed Jericho Wall invasion plan in 2022. During 2023, female surveillance soldiers repeatedly reported increasingly alarming Hamas exercises and reconnaissance along the Gaza border. A specialist female Unit 8200 analyst warned that the exercises closely matched the invasion plan — yet IDF Military Intelligence dismissed the warnings despite first-hand documented reports. Israel has substantially weakened Hamas, Hezbollah, and Iran's military capabilities, but former senior security commanders warn that government policies threaten another avoidable major confrontation. (Source)
