Key findings

Russia / Ukraine / sanctions

  • Anadolu Agency (Oct 17): Trump issued an executive order implementing the Lindsey Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 — but waived its tariffs and many sanctions; duties on Russian goods raised 500%, plus an additional 100% duty on the five largest importers of Russian oil and gas making new purchases on/after Oct 18.
  • CNN (Oct 18): Trump waives Russia-related sanctions before they kick in, drawing backlash from Ukraine and US lawmakers; EU leaders expressed regret over the temporary lift on Russian diesel sanctions. Zelensky rejected Trump's "energy ceasefire" and said he felt "betrayed" (Le Monde).
  • CNN (Oct 16): Ukraine runs out of Patriot interceptor missiles as Zelensky and Trump clash over the war; Zelensky said Ukraine will resume strikes on Russian refineries.
  • CNN (Oct 18): Ukraine defies Trump and launches one of its biggest drone attacks on Russia this year.
  • UN DPPA: 100+ civilians killed and 429+ injured in Ukraine in one week (OHCHR); growing damage to energy facilities ahead of winter.
  • Anadolu: Putin said "security zone" plans on the Ukraine border will not be subject to peace negotiations.

Middle East

  • Anadolu (Oct 17): Strait of Hormuz shipping traffic ~70% below pre-conflict levels; 12 attacks on vessels in one week, including a container ship struck near Ras Shaykh Masud Oct 13 that caught fire and sank.
  • FT: Oil hit a five-month high of $110/barrel on the escalation; Houthis encircling Taiz (Yemen's largest cities), attacking Saudi Aramco HQ and Riyadh airport; EU-Gulf meeting and Formula 1 race postponed.
  • CNN (Oct 17): Israeli settlers attacked European diplomats in the West Bank; Belgian foreign minister said Belgium will press for EU sanctions over the violence. WaPo photographer Heidi Levine injured in West Bank attack — US State Dept "greatly concerned."
  • CNN (Oct 18): Nine days before Israeli elections, Trump endorses Netanyahu.
  • UN DPPA (Oct 9): Special Envoy Hans Grundberg warned the Security Council that Yemen has returned to full-scale war.

Great-power competition: US-China-Taiwan

  • WEF: Xi's Washington visit delivered ceremony but minimal strategic progress — reciprocal tariff cuts on ~$30B of non-sensitive trade only; tariff truce extended two months to January; semiconductors, EVs, batteries excluded; Taiwan discussed but omitted from official summit documents.
  • Charter97 / Bild (Oct 18): NATO SG Mark Rutte warned if Xi attacks Taiwan he would likely ask Putin to "distract" the US by attacking NATO territory — "probably exactly what will happen"; called the moment the most dangerous since WWII.
  • The Daily Star (Oct 18): Russia Deputy FM Rudenko accused the US of using Taiwan to provoke a crisis in Asia; reaffirmed Moscow's backing of Beijing's position.
  • Focus Taiwan (Oct 17): NSC Secretary-General Joseph Wu urged closer Taiwan-Europe ties, citing NATO's report that China is the "decisive enabler" of Russia's war; listed hybrid threats: military drills, undersea cable sabotage, election interference.
  • Taiwan News: US Congress moves to codify the Reagan-era Six Assurances (H.R. 3452 / S. 3208); Japan-Philippines Reciprocal Access Agreement in force; Australia signed for three Japanese Mogami-class frigates; Lai pledged 5% of GDP defense spending by 2030. First two F-16V Block 70 fighters arrived Oct 2.
  • Anadolu: The US became the first nation to acknowledge deploying weapons in Earth's orbit; suspected explosives found near RAF Fairford (US ops hub in UK).

Regional / other

  • Al Jazeera: Russia's Interior Minister Kolokoltsev met Pakistan army chief Munir in Rawalpindi (Oct 14); Moscow positioning as mediator between Kabul and Islamabad (Zamir Kabulov, TASS).
  • Le Monde: France summoned Iran's ambassador over a "disinformation campaign" on the French school protests; Macron vowed to hold accountable those who "sought to bring down the Republic."
  • WEF: Denmark, Greenland and the US signed an Arctic security agreement expanding US military presence while reaffirming Danish sovereignty — after Trump's earlier call for US control of Greenland.
  • UN DPPA: 81st UN General Assembly session held amid "the highest level of active conflict since WWII."
