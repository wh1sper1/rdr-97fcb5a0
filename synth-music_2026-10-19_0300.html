Key findings

TRENDING: Teenage Engineering founder says he's quitting synths

  • GearNews / Push and Groove (October 8, 2026): Teenage Engineering's founder says he plans to quit building synthesizers, OP-1 included. The news sent shockwaves through the synth community — here's what we know and what it could mean for your gear. This is the biggest synth-industry story of the month and dominated forum and social-media discussion across the scene. (Source)

TRENDING: M-VAVE FM-1 frenzy and the firmware hacking boom

  • GearNews (October 2026): The M-VAVE FM-1 is truly the synth of the moment — sound designers are making hay while the sun shines, releasing presets and sounds for the cheap FM synth. A new browser-based emulator called fm1-emu lets you play the M-VAVE FM-1 synthesizer and explore custom firmware without owning the hardware (released by Joakim Eriksson). An ecosystem of open solutions is emerging around the FM-1 — you'll soon be able to preview synths online, download them to cheap hardware, or run them as plugins in your DAW. A new firmware even turns the FM-1 into a pocket Omnichord. (Source)
  • Push and Groove (October 2026): Elektron has responded to the growing wave of alternative firmware for its instruments — a significant statement from the Swedish hardware maker as unofficial OS builds change sequencing, sound engines, and overall behavior. New third-party hacks dropped for Sequential Prophet-5, Arturia MicroFreak (Superfreaker adds Drive, Lo-Fi, tape echo, separate Release, Legato Glide, metronome, transpose, Shift latch), and Novation Circuit Tracks. Free firmware mod Model-TG adds a full Sampler machine and more to the Elektron Model:Cycles. Unofficial firmware brings Outbox 8 support to Elektron OctraTrack. (Source)
  • Synthtopia (October 2026): Preview M-Vave FM-1 alternate firmware options in your web browser. Alternate firmware for the Arturia Microfreak Superfreaker is now available. A free firmware mod adds "incredible" features to the Elektron Model:Cycles. (Synthtopia also covered the first jam on the new Future Retro 777 bassline synthesizer — a reissue of the classic bassline synth.) (Source)

TRENDING: Moog Matriarch discontinued, Behringer JN-80 Juno clone

  • Push and Groove (October 2026): Dealers worldwide indicate that the Moog Matriarch semi-modular analog synthesizer has been discontinued. Behringer's JN-80, an eight-voice analog clone of the Roland Juno-60, is now available for pre-order in the EU. Teenage Engineering is at work on a battery-powered portable amp called EP-35 K.O.-AMP. The OP-XY received firmware update 1.2.0 adding multi-channel USB, a new drum synth, a dynamic filter, and detune. (Source)

Gear news roundup

  • GearNews (October 15-18, 2026): Sim'N Tonic updated its U-SYNC plugin for the Nome II MIDI clock to version 2.0, adding latency auto-detect, presets, and support for more DAWs. The SQ8L Revival brings back an old favorite. Custom firmware is still booming: three new third-party hacks for Sequential, Arturia, and Novation instruments. (Source)

Synthpop, darkwave, and synthwave releases — October 2026

  • Synthpopworld (October 17, 2026): Munich darkwave duo Rue Oberkampf return with Wehmut — six songs of forest, sea, and longing on Young. Amsterdam darkwave artist Subject Sue presses debut EP Details In The Dark to limited Avant! vinyl. Honduran synthwave producer Paradise86 returns with Girl with Glasses, a two-track single out October 17. (Source)
  • Synthpopworld (October 16, 2026): Swedish dark synthpop duo Octolab release Waiting for a better ending, their first album for Rexius Records. Arcade High returns with a self-titled synthwave album — its most personal record yet. Kid Neon's eight-track Digital Bouquet arrives on My Pet Flamingo with City Lights. Cassetter & Cyberwalker expand After The Rush with eight new remixes. The Human League's Dare, the Martin Rushent-produced synthpop landmark, turns 45 on October 16. (Source)
  • Synthpopworld (October 15, 2026): DROIDGLOW returns to NewRetroWave with Brutal Hours, an eleven-track darksynth album with guests including House of... 3FORCE release Strange Peace, a ten-track synthwave album on FiXT gathering seven singles and three new tracks. Das Mörtal releases DRAIN ME, a dark synthwave single with a Dan Terminus remix. Dead Astronauts release Shallow Grave, a fifth album pulling from darkwave, synthwave, and post-punk. Finnish coldwave and synthpunk artist Suzi Sabotage releases Sacrifice on Out Of Line Music, a 130 BPM track. (Source)
  • Synthpopworld (October 13, 2026): Berlin synthpop duo WHOLE release THING, their third album on Blackjack Illuminist Records, mixing industrial and darkwave. Berlin trio Dina Summer return with Children Of The Night, a nine-track mini album on Iptamenos Discos. Swedish dark synth act Priest sets fifth album Altered State Police for October 23 on Blue. Deine Lakaien singer Alexander Veljanov returns as Veljanov with Nomos Shanderilan. (Source)
  • Synthpopworld (October 2026): Belgian synthpop acts Infrablack and Nevel released No Angel on October 9, reworking a 2001 Propulsion track. Spoons announce Nightlife, their eighth album, due November 6. Apoptygma Berzerk's 2016 instrumental album Exit Popularity Contest comes to streaming services October 27. GUNSHIP release Monster in Paradise (Makeup and Vanity Set Remix). Deep Structure release Clash of the Universe, an 11-track album setting '80s sci-fi atmosphere against analogue synths. (Source)
